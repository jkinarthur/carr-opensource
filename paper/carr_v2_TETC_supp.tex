\section{Extended Derivations}
\subsection{Bilevel Gradient Approximation}
Let
\begin{equation}
F(\phi)=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{val}}\big(\theta^*(\phi),\lambda(\phi)\big), \quad
\theta^*(\phi)=\arg\min_{\theta}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{train}}(\theta,\lambda(\phi)).
\end{equation}
By implicit differentiation,
\begin{equation}
\nabla_\phi F
=\partial_\phi \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{val}} +
\partial_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{val}}\cdot
\frac{d\theta^*}{d\phi},
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
\frac{d\theta^*}{d\phi}= -\big(\nabla^2_{\theta\theta}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{train}}\big)^{-1}
\nabla^2_{\theta\phi}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{train}}.
\end{equation}
The first-order update used in practice replaces $\theta^*(\phi)$ with one inner step,
introducing bias of order $O(\xi^2)$ under smoothness assumptions.

\subsection{Critical-Depth Stabilization Intuition}
The training objective trades geometry/evidence terms against efficiency terms. Locally around
$k^*$, one obtains a sign change in the depth derivative, yielding a stable neighborhood
for the learned soft depth rather than a global optimality guarantee.

\section{Leakage Audit Protocol}
The strict Steam audit script checks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Train/validation overlap at the exact $(\mathrm{sequence}, \mathrm{target})$ level.
  \item Candidate-set integrity for sampled-negative evaluation (single positive, no positive in negatives).
  \item Full-catalog vs sampled-negative consistency checks.
  \item Optional shuffled-target sanity test for checkpointed models.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Observed audit outcome.}
The executed Steam audit reported zero train/validation overlap windows,
candidate-integrity pass, sampled-vs-full consistency pass for fixed and
learnable checkpoints, shuffled-target sanity pass for both checkpoints,
and overall\_pass $=\,$true.

\section{Open-Weight Baseline Expansion Status}
We executed open-weight baseline runs (mistral-7b and qwen-7b) on the same EC2 g5.xlarge
instance (22~GB GPU VRAM) to extend the open-model comparison scope. Table~\ref{tab:open_weight_status}
documents the complete attempt across all four datasets.

Mistral-7b successfully evaluated on ML-1M and Beauty datasets, yielding HR@10 of 0.0625 and 0.0938
respectively. Qwen-7b encounters CUDA out-of-memory errors immediately after Mistral completes on each
dataset, preventing Toys and Steam from executing. The 22~GB GPU constraint with $\sim$14~GB per model
load leaves insufficient headroom for sequential multi-model evaluation. This limitation is documented
here for full transparency; future work may benefit from larger GPU resources or model quantization strategies.
